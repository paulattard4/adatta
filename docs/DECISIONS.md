| D15 | Attack/defence prior structure (spec §5.1 deviation) | independent attack and defence (spec) / strength + openness with separate scales | strength + openness | resolved 2026-11-06, pending test: the §14.5 dispersion PPC failed for the Championship. The cause is that predicted totals spread too much between matches (actual-on-predicted slope 0.53; 0.15–0.31 in second tiers): openness = attack − defence (how high-scoring a team's games are) is fitted as a lasting trait but is mostly noise.
